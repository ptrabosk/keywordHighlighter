Privacy and usage data

The extension checks your Chrome profile email locally to verify your organization. It does not upload that email or message text.

You may optionally allow anonymous usage and diagnostic events: timestamps, random IDs, extension version, surface, error details, and highlight counts. Events are sent over HTTPS to the publisher's Google Apps Script receiver and deleted after 90 days.
